# Privacy, Access & Governance Proposal

| Role | Access |
|---|---|
| Engineering | Event schemas and technical diagnostics |
| Data Analyst | Approved pseudonymized analytical datasets |
| Product / Operations | Aggregated business reporting |
| Finance | Restricted wallet and withdrawal reconciliation |
| Intern | Approved/sanitized datasets only |

Apply least privilege, purpose limitation, data minimization, pseudonymous IDs, audit logging, approved retention/deletion rules and controlled exports. Keep passwords, API keys, payment credentials and authentication secrets out of analytics datasets. Legal basis, exact retention and access policies require organization/security/legal approval.
